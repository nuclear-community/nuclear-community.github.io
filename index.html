The learning path.

Eight modules, in order — each one a self-contained guide with key concepts, free resources, and self-check questions.

  1. 01Nuclear physics foundationsAtoms, nuclei, binding energy, radioactive decay›
  2. 02Nuclear reactions & cross sectionsNeutron interactions, fission, fusion›
  3. 03Reactor physicsModeration, criticality, reactor kinetics›
  4. 04Thermal-hydraulicsHeat generation, coolants, plant systems›
  5. 05Radiation detection & protectionDetectors, dosimetry, shielding, ALARA›
  6. 06Fuel cycle & wasteMining, enrichment, spent fuel, disposal›
  7. 07Safety, security & safeguardsDefense in depth, PSA, licensing, nonproliferation›
  8. 08Applications & societyReactors & SMRs, medicine, policy, communication›

What we cover.

Nuclear physics

Atomic & nuclear structure, radioactivity, decay chains, fission and fusion.

Reactor physics

Neutron transport, moderation, criticality, kinetics, burnup and poisons.

Thermal-hydraulics

Heat generation and removal, coolant behavior, passive safety systems.

Radiation protection

Detection, dosimetry, shielding, and the ALARA principle in practice.

Fuel cycle & waste

From mining and enrichment to spent fuel management and disposal.

Safety & society

Safety culture, regulation, nonproliferation, and public communication.
